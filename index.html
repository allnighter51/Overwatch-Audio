How to View & Use in OBS Studio

1. Connect Spotify (Optional): Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard, copy your Client ID (or Access Token), and paste it in Section 2. Every time a song starts or changes, the visualizer lines will collapse to the center and pan out to reveal the new track!

2. Export Your Clean Overlay: Customize your song title, Spotify key, audio source, and X/Y screen offset, then click Export Clean OBS Overlay (.HTML). It strips out all menus, background, and alignment guides automatically while keeping your Spotify connection active.

3. Add to OBS Studio:

  • In OBS, click + under Sources and select Browser.
  • Check Local file and select your downloaded `ow2_hud_addon_overlay.html` file (or point URL to ?obs=1).
  • Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080.
  • Place it above your Overwatch 2 Game Capture—the visualizer sits right next to your real health bar and the song ticker sits right underneath your name!
